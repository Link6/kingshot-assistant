## 中文

- 王城补兵新增每位敌方车头独立的宠物估计计时，按 2 小时持续时间计算。
- 可填写估计已开启多久；未知时填 0，并明确提示实际可能更早结束。
- 预计结束前 5 分钟显示黄色提醒，提醒更新行军时间并重新记录攻击。支持声音及桌面后台提醒。
- 宠物计时会保存到本机，刷新、切换语言和重新记录攻击不会重置；可重新估计或标记已结束。

Windows 提供安装版和免安装便携版 EXE；macOS 提供 ARM64 DMG；Linux 提供 AppImage。附 SHA-256 校验文件。应用尚未签名或公证，需要手动更新。

[在线使用](https://link6.github.io/kingshot-assistant/#guard) · [使用说明](https://github.com/Link6/kingshot-assistant/blob/v0.2.4/README.md)

## English

- Added an independent estimated pet timer for each enemy leader in Reinforcements, using a 2-hour duration.
- Enter estimated elapsed minutes, or 0 when unknown. The interface makes clear that the effect may end earlier.
- A yellow reminder appears 5 minutes before the estimated end, prompting you to update march times and record attacks again. Sound and desktop background reminders are supported.
- Estimates are saved locally and survive reloads, language changes, and new attack recordings. Revise the estimate or mark the effect as ended at any time.

Downloads include Windows installer and portable EXEs, a macOS ARM64 DMG, a Linux AppImage, and SHA-256 checksums. Builds are unsigned and not notarized; updates must be installed manually.
