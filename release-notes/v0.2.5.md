# Kingshot Assistant v0.2.5

## 简体中文

- 集结同步新增每位车头独立的宠物估计计时，按 2 小时计算，预计结束前 5 分钟提醒核对行军时间并重新生成排程。
- 提醒按钮可跳到对应车头的行军时间输入框；刷新、切换语言或重新生成排程均保留宠物计时。
- 集结和防守宠物提醒分别显示，桌面版支持两组各 30 位车头的宠物后台提醒。

## English

- Added independent pet estimate timers for rally leaders: a 2-hour duration with a reminder 5 minutes before the estimated end to check march times and regenerate the schedule.
- The reminder jumps to the affected leader's march input. Reloading, language changes, and schedule regeneration preserve pet timers.
- Rally and defense reminders are displayed separately. Desktop supports pet reminders for up to 30 leaders in each tool.
